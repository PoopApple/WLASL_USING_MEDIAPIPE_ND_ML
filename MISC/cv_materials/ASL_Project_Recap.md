# American Sign Language Recognition — Project Recap

Built a landmark-based ASL word recognizer that converts WLASL GTE9 videos into normalized MediaPipe Holistic sequences (70 frames × 63 landmarks × 4 features) and classifies 171 words using compact, regularized sequence models (BiGRU / lightweight BiLSTM) with 8× augmentation. The best BiGRU run achieved 49.71% top‑1 and 78.65% top‑5 accuracy (train accuracy 93.26%, loss 2.998), with a reproducible end‑to‑end pipeline covering landmark extraction, normalization, training, and evaluation. Artifacts such as training curves, confusion matrices, and top‑5 predictions are saved under testing/model_comparison_results. Future scope includes expanding the dataset (more samples and signer diversity), incorporating facial landmarks, adding velocity/angle features and hand‑shape descriptors, exploring TCN/1D‑CNN+RNN hybrids and transfer learning, and optimizing for real‑time/mobile deployment.
